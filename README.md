# NNT or NextNewTab

> **Unify, debloat your New Tab on desktop or mobile in any browser that support extension.**

Turn every new tab into a clean, personal launchpad for the sites you
use most. **Next New Tab** brings your shortcuts, browsing essentials,
and visual customization together in one focused page---so your next
click is always close at hand.

## ✨ Features

-   **Quick-access tiles** --- keep your favorite websites within easy
    reach.
-   **Recent visits** --- get back to pages you visited recently.
-   **Bookmarks and top sites** --- bring useful browser destinations
    into your new-tab workflow.
-   **Browser-powered search** --- search using your browser's
    configured search provider.
-   **Clock and search visibility controls** --- show or hide these
    elements to suit your setup.
-   **Adjustable tile size** --- tune the layout to your preference.
-   **Centered tile grids** --- keep the page balanced as your layout
    changes.
-   **Wallpaper browsing** --- preview available wallpapers and load
    more choices.
-   **Settings backup** --- export and import supported settings so you
    can restore your preferences.
-   **Lightweight interface** --- built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript,
    with attention to responsive interactions and efficient updates.

*The exact features available may depend on the version you install and
the browser APIs supported by your browser.*

## 🌐 Support

Next New Tab is designed as a browser extension for Chromium-based
browsers such as **Google Chrome**, with Firefox compatibility being
tested and refined. You can use this in mobile and desktop also.

## 📦 Installation

### Google Chrome

1.  Download the extension ZIP from the project's **Releases** page, or
    use the project files.
2.  Extract the ZIP into a folder.
3.  Open `chrome://extensions` in Chrome.
4.  Turn on **Developer mode**.
5.  Select **Load unpacked** and choose the extracted extension folder.
6.  Open a new tab to try Next New Tab.

## ⚙️ Customization

Open the extension's settings to adjust the options available in your
installed version, such as tile size, visibility of the search bar or
clock, and supported settings import/export.

## 🔐 Permissions and privacy

The extension requests browser permissions for features such as
bookmarks, top sites, browsing history, and storage. It may also use
host access for website favicons or metadata, depending on the feature.

-   Permissions are used to provide the extension's features.
-   Review the permission prompts shown by your browser before
    installing.
-   Wallpaper images may be loaded from external image hosts, so those
    requests can reveal normal connection information to the image host.
-   Do not assume that all data stays local unless the source code and
    network behavior have been reviewed for the specific release.
